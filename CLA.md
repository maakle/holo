## How to sign

Pull requests are gated by a CLA bot. The first time You open a PR you'll be prompted to
sign by commenting on the PR — the bot records Your GitHub username and the date.

If You're contributing on behalf of a company, ask Your employer to sign a Corporate CLA
(open an issue tagged `cla-corporate` and we'll send the form).
